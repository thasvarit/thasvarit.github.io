Education

I study Physics and Computer Science at Chulalongkorn University. I switched over from Engineering after the 2024 Nobel Prize in Physics, awarded for the physics behind neural networks, showed me how closely the two fields connect.

Work

Now

I want to use AI to help people through healthcare, so I’m taking courses in computational biology and machine learning for biomedicine. Meanwhile, I work as a freelance Data & AI Engineer for the Bangkok Metropolitan Administration, where I lead a small team building analytics and automation tools.

Quantitative Finance Since 2024

I was the first president of QUANT CU, Chulalongkorn University’s quant club, where I worked on financial machine learning and portfolio optimization. I also translate quantitative trading books into Thai for MPIRIKA Books, including Paul Wilmott’s The Mathematics of Finance for High Schoolers.

Aerospace Engineering Until 2022

I was a simulation engineer at SPACE AC Institute of Technology, working on rocket trajectory optimization. My team won 1st place at the US CanSat Competition, hosted by the American Astronautical Society.
